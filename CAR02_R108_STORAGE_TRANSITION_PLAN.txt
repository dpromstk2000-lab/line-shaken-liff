- Standard Storage remove takes object paths, not a proven atomic path+SHA+version condition.
- Uploads with upsert:false reject an already-existing path, but upsert:true may overwrite it.
- Backend secret/service-role authorization can bypass database RLS; enforcing a writer allowlist requires reviewing ALL credentials and privileged workflows, not just app-level RLS.
- Storage metadata must not be deleted directly from SQL. Use Storage API after a reviewed process.

RECOMMENDED INTERIM BEHAVIOR: QUARANTINE_ONLY
- On ambiguous upload or delete result, do NOT immediately invoke unconditional path DELETE.
- Durable recovery request is queued for manual/approved recovery, without disclosing photos or token values in logging.
- Mark the photo unavailable to normal viewers until its state can be independently reconciled; do not claim physical deletion.
- A cleanup worker may not promote requests to actual DELETE until an independently reviewed isolated staging proof, exact tenant/path/actor authorization, audit trail, and disaster-recovery approval exist.
- The R108 JavaScript is an in-process model; it does NOT write a durable queue or change R47. It cannot be deployed.
